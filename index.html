Before you decide

Practical questions.
Direct answers.

Can I have veneers without shaving my teeth?

Some teeth can suit a no-prep design; others need preparation. Your dentist assesses the available space, tooth colour, gums and bite before recommending an approach. No-prep cannot be promised before examination.

Will they make my teeth look bulky?

The final contour depends on the veneer and the original tooth. If adding a shell would create unsuitable bulk, your dentist should discuss preparation or another treatment.

Are zirconia veneers better than glass ceramic?

There is no single best material for every case. Glass-ceramic veneers can also be thin. Material selection should consider the intended appearance, preparation, bonding and maintenance, rather than a material name alone.

Can I finish during a short trip to Bangkok?

Ask the clinic to confirm the schedule after assessment. Allow time for fabrication, try-in, possible adjustments and review. A fixed visit count or completion date cannot be promised from this page.

What happens when I return to Australia?

Discuss your review plan and how to contact the clinic before travelling home. Keep regular dental reviews where you live. Ask what treatment information can be provided for your dentist.

Are thin veneers reversible or permanent?

If tooth structure is removed, that change is permanent. Veneers may need rebonding, repair or replacement. Even no-prep veneers should not be assumed to be removable without risk to the tooth.
